About

Shuai Xiao is an Associate Professor, Specially Appointed Researcher, and Ph.D. Supervisor in the Department of Information and Communication Engineering at Tianjin University. He received his Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering from Tianjin University in 2022.

His research focuses on artificial intelligence, image generation and editing, medical image processing, and sample informativeness assessment. He has published more than 40 papers as first or corresponding author in leading journals and conferences, including ICLR, AAAI, ACM Multimedia, IEEE Transactions on Multimedia, and IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security.

Research Focus

  • Generative AI and perceptual modeling
  • Deepfake and media manipulation detection
  • Medical image analysis
  • Data quality and sample information measurement
