Brief Bio

I am an Associate Professor, Specially Appointed Researcher, and Ph.D. Supervisor at Tianjin University. I received my Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering from Tianjin University in 2022.

My research interests include artificial intelligence, image generation and editing, medical image processing, and sample informativeness assessment. I have published more than 40 papers as first or corresponding author.
