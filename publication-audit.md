# Publication audit — Shuai Xiao homepage

Reviewed 30 September 2026. This is a verification record for the 32 publications shown on the site, not a claim that every paper is itself “top” within its venue. The inclusion rule is publication year 2021 or later **and** either a journal in JCR JIF Q1 (in at least one category) / CAS Zone 1, or a full paper at a CCF A conference in the 2026 seventh edition. A journal's quartile can change by year and category; the source and year used are noted below. Author position is not an inclusion rule.

## Open author-role items

The page uses an asterisk only for seven supported positive attributions. Five provisional asterisks were removed: #1 because the paper contradicts it, and #5, #8, #17, #18 because the original corresponding-author notes were not accessible. A sixth (#12) was removed because only a non-publisher facsimile exposed the marker. The remaining unmarked roles must **not** be read as verified negative. To establish a complete yes/no matrix, obtain each publisher PDF's first page or the authors' accepted manuscript and inspect the correspondence footnote, then have Professor Xiao confirm the final list.
